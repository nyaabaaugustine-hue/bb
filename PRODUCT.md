# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
People in Ghana looking to rent, buy, build, or invest in property.

## Product Purpose
B B Homes helps people explore housing options and move through the journey from their first enquiry to move-in and ongoing home care. Success means visitors understand their next step and can contact a named team member with confidence.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, welcoming, grounded.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly playful, flashy, or distracting visual treatments and animations.

## Design Principles
- Make each housing step and its next action easy to understand.
- Earn trust with clear information, named contacts, and visible processes.
- Keep interactions welcoming and grounded in the realities of buying, renting, and building.
- Let motion support comprehension rather than compete with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Honor reduced-motion preferences. Keep all steps and their relationships understandable without animation.
